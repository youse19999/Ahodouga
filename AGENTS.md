# Agent instructions

## Hotline safety policy

Before processing or presenting any video or user generated content in this project, read `HOTLINE.md` and `video.json` together. Apply the safety guidance in `HOTLINE.md` and inspect the `IsNeedHotline` field in `video.json` as part of that review. Do not infer a person's condition from the flag alone; follow the project's documented handling and offer support resources only when appropriate.
